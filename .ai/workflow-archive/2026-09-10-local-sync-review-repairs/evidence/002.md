## Main-thread review

Reviewed operation ownership, socket interruption, result delivery, and every persistence/import boundary. Pairing workers now have one cancellation token, receiver, and join handle owned by `PairingRuntime`; lock, quit, replacement, drop, and explicit cancellation synchronously tear them down. Completed workers are joined when polled. The cancellation token tracks the live socket so an established blocking handshake or vault fetch does not wait for the ordinary network deadline. Existing-vault confirmation no longer performs a redundant third registry write after the active state was already persisted by the transaction callback.
