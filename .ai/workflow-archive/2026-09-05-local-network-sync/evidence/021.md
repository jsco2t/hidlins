## Main-thread review

The live investigation isolated two independent emulator behaviors: Android platform NSD successfully advertised and resolved the authority route, while an authority process borrowed from the force-stopped/background mobile application UID could not accept inbound traffic. The reviewed repair runs the real CLI as the standalone guest authority represented by the scenario and leaves the Flutter application client-only. A cleanup review also moved registrar cleanup arming before registration startup so partial startup and timeout paths unregister explicitly. No endpoint is supplied to the client and no mobile server was introduced.
